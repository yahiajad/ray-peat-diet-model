# Codebook: Ray Peat dietary rules (primary-source only)

Compiled 2026-10-03. Every rule below is taken from Peat's own words: (a) articles on raypeat.com (his own site), and (b) interview transcripts hosted at raypeat.rodeo (GitHub: marcuswhybrow/ray-peat-rodeo), which are **Whisper AI auto-transcripts without speaker labels** of radio shows (Ask Your Herb Doctor/KMUD, East West Healing, Politics & Science, Gary Null 1996, etc.). Interview wording can contain ASR errors (e.g. "keratin" for carotene, "class" for glass); each such risk is flagged. Interview audio original = mirror links in each raypeat.rodeo page. **Newsletters were NOT accessed** (paywalled/not publicly mirrored in this pass) - see Gaps.

**Counts:** 94 rules: **48 QUANT** (he gave a number) / **46 QUAL** (direction only).

Verification: each URL was fetched (HTTP 200) and each quote string-matched on the page (case/punctuation-insensitive). Status column shows result per rule.


## Crux summary for the adequacy model

| Parameter | Peat's stated position | Rules |
|---|---|---|
| Protein | 70-100 g/day minimum (many interviews 80-100; 120-150 for active; own ~120 g in 2016). **Late change 2022: ~50 g/day with 400-600 g carbohydrate.** | P01-P07, P10 |
| Milk | >=2 qt/day (range 2 qt - 1 gal), 1% preferred; 2 qt milk = 66 g protein, >2,000 mg Ca. Late 2022: limit milk/cheese/eggs initially. | P09, M01-M08 |
| Calcium | 1,500 mg minimum; ~2,000-2,500 mg own/target; eggshell/oyster-shell as supplement | C01, C02, P05 |
| Ca:P | Very high; phosphate <= 2x calcium (strict) or 2-3x (relaxed with good sugar); milk/cheese 1.3:1; meat up to 10:1; cap meat+grain+legume ~0.5 lb | C03-C08 |
| Iron | Harmful in excess; avoid fortified flour/cereal/pasta; no RDA offered; coffee with meat, no OJ with iron-rich meal; milk+eggs diet gives enough | I01-I09 |
| Vitamin A | Preformed retinol (liver, egg yolk, cod/halibut liver oil), not carotene; liver ~6 oz weekly (later far less) | A01-A03, L01-L04 |
| Fibre | Only "safe" fibre: one raw carrot/day, cooked potato, ~1 cup mushrooms; cook all other vegetables ~40 min; no g/day fibre target | K01-K06, V03 |
| Sugar/sucrose | Favoured over starch; OJ 1-2 qt/day; "a few hundred g" carbohydrate; 500-600 g in 2022 | S01-S07, M01, M02 |
| Starch | Tolerated only if thoroughly cooked with butter; potato favoured over grains/beans | S05, T01-T03 |

**I04 | Coffee with meat | QUAL**

**I05 | Orange juice timing | QUAL**

- Quote: "Vitamin C stimulates the absorption of iron, so it might be a good idea to avoid drinking orange juice at the same meal with iron-rich foods."

**I06 | Copper foods | QUAL**

## Contradictions and changes over time (consolidated)

1. **Protein collapse (2022):** 70-150 g/day (P01-P05, 1990s-2020) -> ~50 g/day with 400-600 g carbohydrate (P06, P07, S06), justified by methionine/cysteine/tryptophan restriction and mTOR. In the 2022-06-25 interview he says the earlier "Peat diet" was never defined in macronutrients and 100 g was relative to 4-5,000 kcal intakes.
2. **Milk/liver/eggs reversal (2022):** 2-4 qt milk, weekly liver, 1-3+ eggs (M03-M05, L01, E01-E02) -> "somewhat limit" milk/cheese/eggs, chicken liver ~every two months (M07, L04).
3. **OJ quantity:** 1 qt (2012 article, M01) vs 2 qt (2008 interview, M02) vs "at least a pint" own habit (M04) vs up to a gallon (2022).
4. **Ca:P ratio strictness:** "very high" / <=2:1 (C03, C04) vs 2-3:1 acceptable with good sugar intake (C05) vs "isn't as big an issue as a lot of books say" (2011-12-16 interview, quote not coded because speaker could not be confirmed).
5. **Eggs:** 1-2/day (2008, PUFA concern) -> 2-3 (2019) -> 4-5 with known free-range eggs (2019-04-29).
6. **Orange juice vs iron:** advises OJ in large amounts yet says avoid OJ with iron-rich meals and that daily liver + OJ gives toxic iron (I05, I07).
7. **Grains:** "totally give up bread and pasta" (1996, V01) vs white flour/rice "nutritionally" advantageous over whole grain (2012, V02).
8. **Olive oil:** tablespoon/day fine (2008-12) vs teaspoon-scale/"not as good as butter" (2008-07/08) (O04). Palm oil: listed toxic (O06) vs separated palm fraction counted saturated (2009-09-01 interview).
9. **Cod/halibut liver oil** accepted as vitamin A source (A01) while fish oil is "worse than seed oils" (O07).
10. **Coconut oil:** ~1 oz/day (article) vs "up to three tablespoons" (2011).
